IoT / WIRELESS SENSING

Wi-Fi CSI-Based Human Presence and Movement Detection

Developing a portable home-safety monitoring system that uses Wi-Fi Channel State Information (CSI) to detect human presence and movement without relying on cameras or microphones.

The prototype architecture combines ESP32-based wireless sensing with Raspberry Pi processing and a touchscreen interface.

Development Highlights

  • Configured ESP32 transmitter and receiver hardware.
  • Successfully flashed and verified wireless sensing firmware.
  • Established the foundation for CSI-based sensing and processing.
  • Designed for initial single-room movement detection, with future mobile notifications planned.
